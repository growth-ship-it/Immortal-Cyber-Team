# Immortal Website Design System

## Color and effects

- Use the Immortal yellow and `#070D11` dark as the default interaction colors.
- Do not introduce blue gradients, decorative color gradients, or colored glow effects.
- Hover and focus states must use flat color changes to fills, borders, text, or icons. Do not use colored glow shadows.
- Keep motion restrained. Interactive elements may scale subtly, but must not jump or move upward on hover.
